DOOMSCROLL TOGETHER

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 27, 2026

Doomscroll Together is an App for Root communities. Members share short videos into one feed, scroll it together, chat and react. This policy explains what the App stores, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how to ask for it to be deleted.

What we store

The App stores the following in the community's App data on Root's servers:

  • Shared reels: the video link, its title, thumbnail and creator name as published on the original site, the Root user ID of the member who shared it, when it was shared, the Root user IDs of members who liked it, and how many reactions it received.
  • Chat messages: the message text, the Root user ID of the sender and the time it was sent.
  • The queue: which reels are queued, the Root user ID of the member who queued each one and when.
  • Member stats: for members who share or react, their Root user ID, their sharing streak (the last day they shared and how many days in a row), how many reels they have shared and reactions they have sent, used for streaks and badges like "Top Sharer".
  • Watch parties: the party name and start time, the Root user ID of the member who planned it, and the Root user IDs of members who asked to be reminded.
  • Notification choices: the Root user IDs of members who opened the App (so the App knows whom to notify) and which notification types each member turned off.
  • Moderation: when a moderator mutes a member's chat, removes them from Watch Together or locks their posting, the member's Root user ID and when that ends.
  • App settings that community admins enter, such as moderator, poster, banned and party-planner roles or members, chat on/off, slow mode and extra blocked words and an optional YouTube API key for auto-feed.

Watch Together sessions (who is watching, who is hosting, skip votes and the shared playback position) and individual emoji reactions are shared live and are not saved. Chat messages are checked against a list of blocked words before they are shown, and blocked words are replaced with asterisks; the original wording is not kept. On your own device, the App may remember small display choices, like whether sound is on, the chat ping or whether you minimized a side video.

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts or payment details. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we store it

Only to run the App's features: showing the shared feed and who shared each reel, likes, reactions, chat, the queue, Watch Together, streaks and badges, watch parties and reminders, notifications, moderation, and the settings admins choose. We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Who receives it

  • Members of the same community can see the shared feed, likes, reaction counts, chat messages, the queue, who is watching together, sharing streaks and badges, and planned watch parties, along with members' Root display names. Moderation status (muted, removed or locked) is only shown to the member it applies to.
  • Notifications are sent through Root's notification system to members who have not turned them off, for example when Watch Together starts, when people join, when reels are queued or when a watch party they asked to be reminded about starts. They include display names and reel or party titles.
  • Root hosts the App and stores its data. Root's own privacy policy applies to your Root account.
  • Video sites. Reels play in players embedded from the site they came from: YouTube or TikTok. Those players load directly in your browser, and those sites may collect information under their own privacy policies. When a link is shared, the App's server asks that site for the video's title and thumbnail. If an admin turns on auto-feed, the App's server requests trending YouTube Shorts. These requests do not include your personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Chat: only the most recent 100 messages are kept. Older messages are deleted automatically.
  • Reels: the feed keeps at most 500 reels. The oldest are removed automatically as new ones arrive.
  • Queue: an entry is removed when its reel is played or removed.
  • Watch parties: removed 2 hours after their start time, or when cancelled.
  • Moderation: mutes and Watch Together removals are deleted once they end. Posting locks stay until a moderator unlocks them.
  • Members who opened the App: at most the 5,000 most recent are kept for notifications.
  • Data stays until it is removed this way, deleted by a member or moderator, or deleted on request.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own chat messages and reels you shared at any time from inside the App. Community moderators can also remove them.

To ask for any other data about you to be deleted, or for a copy of it, email us with the name of the Root community and your Root display name. We will respond within 30 days.
