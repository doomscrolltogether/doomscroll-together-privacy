DOOMSCROLL TOGETHER

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 23, 2026

Doomscroll Together is an App for Root communities. Members share short videos into one feed, scroll it together, chat and react. This policy explains what the App stores, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how to ask for it to be deleted.

What we store

The App stores the following in the community's App data on Root's servers:

  • Shared reels: the video link, its title, thumbnail and creator name as published on the original site, the Root user ID of the member who shared it, when it was shared, and the Root user IDs of members who liked it.
  • Chat messages: the message text, the Root user ID of the sender and the time it was sent.
  • The queue: which reels are queued, the Root user ID of the member who queued each one and when.
  • App settings that community admins enter, such as moderator roles, side video links and optional API keys for auto-feed sources.

Watch Together sessions (who is watching) and emoji reactions are shared live and are not saved. On your own device, the App may remember small display choices, like whether you minimized a side video.

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts or payment details. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we store it

Only to run the App's features: showing the shared feed and who shared each reel, likes, chat, the queue, and the settings admins choose. We do not sell or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Who receives it

  • Members of the same community can see the shared feed, likes, chat messages and the queue, along with members' Root display names.
  • Root hosts the App and stores its data. Root's own privacy policy applies to your Root account.
  • Video sites. Reels play in players embedded from the site they came from: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Twitch. Those players load directly in your browser, and those sites may collect information under their own privacy policies. When a link is shared, the App's server asks that site for the video's title and thumbnail. If an admin turns on auto-feed, the App's server requests trending videos from the selected sites. These requests do not include your personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Chat: only the most recent 100 messages are kept. Older messages are deleted automatically.
  • Reels: the feed keeps at most 500 reels. The oldest are removed automatically as new ones arrive.
  • Queue: an entry is removed when its reel is played or removed.
  • Data stays until it is removed this way, deleted by a member or moderator, or deleted on request.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own chat messages and reels you shared at any time from inside the App. Community moderators can also remove them.

To ask for any other data about you to be deleted, or for a copy of it, email us with the name of the Root community and your Root display name. We will respond within 30 days.
